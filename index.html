Good to know

Questions before you go?

A few helpful notes before planning your visit.

Ask about your stay ↗
How do I make a reservation?

Choose your dates and accommodation, then prepare a booking request using the form. This demo does not send reservations to the resort; connect an official contact channel or backend before launch.

Are the displayed rates final?

No. Room rates are sample prices for the prototype. Confirm actual rates, inclusions, extra-person fees, and payment terms with the resort.

Can I book for a family or group?

Use the guest selector and accommodation filters. Actual capacity and group arrangements must be confirmed directly with the resort.

What should I confirm before arrival?

Confirm your booking reference, check-in/check-out times, directions, amenities, cancellation terms, and accepted payment methods.

Does this website confirm availability?

Not yet. This is a front-end concept using sample room data. Real availability requires a connected booking system or staff-managed calendar.
