GOOD TO KNOW

Before you book

Is my booking confirmed when I download the QR pass?

No. The QR is a booking request/pass for staff lookup. The resort must verify availability and payment before confirming your reservation.

Does choosing GCash mean I have paid?

No. Online payment remains pending until the resort verifies the transaction. Never put your GCash PIN or OTP in this website.

Where are my valid ID and selfie stored?

This front-end prototype keeps files in the current browser session for form completion and does not upload them. A production website needs secure backend storage, access controls, a retention policy and consent notice.
